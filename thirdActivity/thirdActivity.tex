The intent of this web document is to demonstrate the advantages of putting your course content on the web.

In addition to normal tex output into an html format, ximera offers the answer box! The answer box is the most fundamental form of assessment feedback. It instantly lets a user know whether their answer was acceptable or not- and it has the patience to allow for as many corrections as are needed.
Let us delay no further, let's meet the answer box:

\begin{problem}
\[
\answer{42}
\]
\end{problem}

Explore the answer box for yourself and notice things about it. Surely you wont actually get the right answer, so you can hack at it all day! Type "infty" or "pi" or "sin(x)"

Ximera offers a Hint mode in addition to the answer box.

\begin{problem}
\begin{hint}
    Your hint goes here
\end{hint}
\begin{hint}
    A second hint if you want- a no second hint button- just a counter added to the hint button: hint 1, hint 2 etc...
\end{hint}

\end{problem}
